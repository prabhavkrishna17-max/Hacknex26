# Jurisdiction Specific Compliance Schedules
**Document ID:** DOC-009
**Version:** 3.0.0
**Classification:** Confidential Commercial Contract
**Domain:** Cross-Jurisdictional Legal Interpretations

## 1. Scope and Precedence of Jurisdiction Schedules
This Document sets forth mandatory statutory adjustments and judicial interpretations applicable when Customer explicitly selects a specific governing law jurisdiction in the Order Form / Master Agreement (DOC-006 Section 11.1). In the event of an irreconcilable conflict between the Master Agreement and an applicable Jurisdiction Schedule, the mandatory rules of the selected Jurisdiction Schedule shall supersede.

## 2. Schedule US-CAL: State of California (United States)
When California is the selected Chosen Jurisdiction:
2.1 **Voidness of Restrictive Covenants:** Pursuant to California Business and Professions Code § 16600 and related judicial precedents, Section 10 of the Master Agreement (Non-Solicitation and Restrictive Covenants) is declared strictly void, unenforceable, and contrary to California public policy with respect to all California-based personnel and contractors.
2.2 **Statutory Limits on Exculpation:** Pursuant to California Civil Code § 1668, contractual provisions that purport to exempt any party from responsibility for its own fraud, willful injury to person or property, or violation of law, whether willful or negligent, are void and of no effect.

## 3. Schedule US-DEL: State of Delaware (United States)
When Delaware is the selected Chosen Jurisdiction:
3.1 **Commercial Freedom of Contract:** Delaware law strictly respects the negotiated allocation of economic risks between sophisticated commercial entities.
3.2 **Enforceability of Restrictive Covenants:** Section 10 (Non-Solicitation) is fully valid and enforceable as a reasonable commercial covenant under Delaware law.
3.3 **Liability Caps and Consequential Damages:** Section 8.1 (Consequential Damages Waiver) and Section 8.2 (12-month Fee Cap) are enforceable to the maximum extent permitted by law, with exceptions limited exclusively to actual intentional fraud pursuant to Section 8.3.

## 4. Schedule UK-ENG: England and Wales (United Kingdom)
When England and Wales is the selected Chosen Jurisdiction:
4.1 **Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (UCTA):** Any provision attempting to exclude or restrict liability for death or personal injury resulting from negligence is void. All other exclusions of liability in Section 8 must satisfy the statutory "requirement of reasonableness" under Section 11 of UCTA.
4.2 **Penalty Doctrine:** Any service credits or liquidated damages under DOC-008 must constitute a proportionate and legitimate business interest under the Supreme Court rule in *Cavendish Square Holding BV v Talal El Makdessi* and shall not constitute an unenforceable penalty.

## 5. Schedule EU-GDPR: European Union Member States
When an EU Member State jurisdiction is selected:
5.1 **Non-Waivable Statutory Liability:** Under GDPR Article 82, statutory rights of data subjects to claim compensation for material or non-material damage cannot be capped or excluded by Section 8 of the Master Agreement.
5.2 **Supervisory Authority Reporting:** In the event of a Personal Data breach, Customer must notify the relevant supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it pursuant to GDPR Article 33.

## 6. Schedule SG: Republic of Singapore
When Singapore is the selected Chosen Jurisdiction:
6.1 **PDPA Breach Notification:** Under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), notification of a notifiable data breach to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) must occur no later than three (3) calendar days after assessing that the breach is notifiable.
6.2 **Third Party Rights:** The Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 (Cap. 53B) shall apply, and no third party shall have rights to enforce any term of this Agreement unless expressly provided.
