## 1. Definitions
As used in this Agreement:
- **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where control means ownership of more than 50% of voting securities.
- **"Applicable Law"** means all local, state, national, and international laws, treaties, regulations, and conventions applicable to the respective Party under the Chosen Jurisdiction.
- **"Confidential Information"** means non-public business, technical, or financial information disclosed by one Party to the other; provided that Confidential Information shall explicitly exclude information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already known to the receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure; or (c) is independently developed by the receiving Party without access to or use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
- **"Customer Data"** means all electronic data, files, and personal records submitted by or on behalf of Customer to the Service, excluding Anonymized Usage Metrics.
- **"Gross Negligence"** means an intentional, conscious, or reckless failure to perform a manifest duty in reckless disregard of the consequences as affecting the life, property, or core rights of another person.
- **"Chosen Jurisdiction"** means the substantive law governing this Agreement pursuant to Section 11.1.

## 2. Services, Provisioning, and SOWs
Vendor shall provide the cloud software services described in mutually executed Statements of Work (SOW). Each SOW constitutes an integral part of this Agreement. In the event of an explicit conflict between this Agreement and any SOW, the terms of this Agreement shall govern unless the SOW explicitly identifies the specific section of this Agreement to be overridden and is signed by an executive officer of both Parties.

## 3. Fees, Invoicing, and Taxes
Customer shall pay all undisputed fees within thirty (30) calendar days of invoice receipt. Overdue balances shall accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory rate permitted by Applicable Law. Customer is solely responsible for all sales, value-added, and consumption taxes, excluding taxes based solely on Vendor's net income.

4.1 **Customer Ownership:** As between the Parties, Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data. Customer grants Vendor a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, process, and transfer Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Services.
4.2 **Vendor Platform IP:** Vendor retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Service, underlying algorithms, documentation, and all improvements and modifications thereto.
4.3 **Feedback License:** If Customer provides feedback or feature requests, Vendor may freely incorporate such feedback without accounting or payment to Customer.

## 5. Confidentiality Obligations and Carveouts
5.1 **Duty of Care:** The receiving Party shall protect disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.
5.2 **Exceptions to Use:** Neither Party shall disclose the other's Confidential Information to any third party except to its Affiliates, legal counsel, and audited contractors who need to know such information and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Section 5.
5.3 **Compelled Disclosure:** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by valid court order or administrative summons; provided that the receiving Party gives prompt written notice (unless legally prohibited) to enable the disclosing Party to seek a protective order.

## 6. Warranties and Sole Remedies
6.1 **Service Warranty:** Vendor warrants that the Services will perform substantially in conformance with the published documentation during the active subscription term.
6.2 **Sole Remedy:** For any breach of the warranty in Section 6.1, Customer's exclusive remedy and Vendor's entire liability shall be the re-performance of the non-conforming Service, or, if Vendor cannot reasonably cure within forty-five (45) days of notice, termination of the affected SOW and a pro-rata refund of prepaid unearned fees.
6.3 **Disclaimer:** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED HEREIN, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS, IMPLIED, OR STATUTORY, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

## 7. Indemnification Obligations
7.1 **Vendor IP Indemnity:** Vendor shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services directly infringe a valid copyright or registered patent of the Chosen Jurisdiction, and shall indemnify Customer against final damages awarded by a competent court; provided that Customer: (a) gives Vendor prompt written notice; (b) grants Vendor sole control of defense and settlement; and (c) provides reasonable cooperation.
7.2 **Carveouts from Vendor Indemnity:** Vendor shall have no liability under Section 7.1 to the extent the infringement claim arises from: (i) combinations with non-Vendor software; (ii) modifications made by Customer; or (iii) Customer Data.
7.3 **Customer Indemnity:** Customer shall defend and indemnify Vendor against third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's violation of Applicable Law.

## 8. Limitation of Liability and Consequential Damages
8.1 **Consequential Damages Waiver:** SUBJECT TO SECTION 8.3, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, PUNITIVE, EXEMPLARY, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, LOSS OF REVENUE, LOSS OF GOODWILL, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS AGREEMENT, REGARDLESS OF THE THEORY OF LIABILITY AND EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.
8.2 **Aggregate Dollar Cap:** SUBJECT TO SECTION 8.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE STRICTLY CAPPED AT THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.
8.3 **Express Carveouts from Liability Caps:** THE LIMITATIONS AND EXCLUSIONS IN SECTION 8.1 AND SECTION 8.2 SHALL NOT APPLY TO:
  (a) EITHER PARTY'S BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 5;

  (c) EITHER PARTY'S GROSS NEGLIGENCE, WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, OR INTENTIONAL FRAUD; OR
  (d) DAMAGES THAT CANNOT BE CONTRACTUALLY LIMITED UNDER APPLICABLE MANDATORY LAW OF THE CHOSEN JURISDICTION.

## 9. Term and Termination
9.1 **Term:** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 9.
9.2 **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches any material term and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) calendar days of receiving written notice specifying the breach.
9.3 **Effect of Termination:** Upon termination, Customer shall cease all use of Services, and each Party shall promptly return or destroy the other's Confidential Information within thirty (30) days, except as required for statutory compliance archives.

## 10. Restrictive Covenants and Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Customer shall not directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or core technical contractor of Vendor who performed services under this Agreement; provided that general public job advertisements not targeted at Vendor personnel shall not constitute a breach of this Section 10.

## 11. Governing Law, Jurisdiction, and Dispute Resolution
11.1 **Governing Law Default:** This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the jurisdiction explicitly selected by the Customer in the Order Form / Schedule of Chosen Jurisdiction, without giving effect to conflicts of laws principles. In the absence of an explicit selection, Delaware law shall apply as commercial default.
11.2 **Dispute Forum:** The state and federal courts located in the Chosen Jurisdiction shall have exclusive jurisdiction over all disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
