# Data Protection Addendum (DPA)
**Document ID:** DOC-007
**Version:** 3.1.0
**Classification:** Confidential Commercial Contract
**Domain:** Enterprise Privacy & Data Protection Compliance

## 1. Scope, Status, and Order of Precedence
This Data Protection Addendum ("DPA") supplements the Master Services Agreement (DOC-006). This DPA governs the processing of Personal Data submitted as Customer Data. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the terms of this DPA and the Agreement (DOC-006), the terms of this DPA shall prevail with respect to data privacy and security of Personal Data.

## 2. Processing Instructions and Roles
2.1 **Roles:** Customer acts as the Data Controller (or Business under CCPA/CPRA), and Vendor acts as the Data Processor (or Service Provider under CCPA/CPRA).
2.2 **Scope of Processing:** Vendor shall process Personal Data solely on Customer's documented lawful instructions and for the specific business purposes set forth in the Agreement, unless otherwise required by Applicable Law.
2.3 **Prohibition on Sale or Sharing:** Vendor shall not sell, retain, use, disclose, or rent Personal Data for any commercial purpose other than the direct provisioning of the Services.

## 3. Security Measures and Incident Notification
3.1 **Technical Safeguards:** Vendor implements industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, including AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
3.2 **Security Incident Notification to Customer:** In the event of a confirmed Personal Data Breach impacting Customer Data, Vendor shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of the incident.
3.3 **Regulatory Reporting Timelines:** Customer acknowledges that reporting obligations to regulatory authorities (such as the EU 72-hour notice under GDPR Article 33 or relevant state attorneys general under CCPA) remain the legal responsibility of Customer as Controller, assisted by Vendor's incident report.

## 4. Sub-processors and Objection Rights
4.1 **Authorized Sub-processors:** Customer grants general written authorization for Vendor to engage third-party sub-processors listed on Vendor's public trust portal.
4.2 **Prior Notice of Changes:** Vendor shall provide at least thirty (30) calendar days prior written notice before onboarding any new sub-processor.
4.3 **Objection Procedure:** Customer may object to a new sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds within ten (10) business days of notice. If Customer objects and Vendor cannot provide an alternative within thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Services without early termination penalty.

## 5. Cross-Border Transfers and Transfer Mechanisms
5.1 **Restricted Transfers:** Transfers of Personal Data from the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, or United Kingdom to countries not deemed adequate by the European Commission shall be governed by the standard contractual clauses (SCCs) set out in Module 2 (Controller-to-Processor) of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.
5.2 **UK Addendum:** For transfers originating from the UK, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA) shall be incorporated by reference.

## 6. Audit Rights and Verification
Customer may, at its own expense and no more than once per calendar year, audit Vendor's data protection compliance upon thirty (30) days prior written notice; provided that Customer accepts an independent SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 audit report in satisfaction of this audit right unless a material security breach occurred in the preceding twelve months.
